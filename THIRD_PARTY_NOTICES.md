## Source delivery and remaining work

GPL components are not automatically incompatible with this AGPL route: [AGPL v3 section 13](https://opensource.org/license/agpl-3.0) and [GPL v3 section 13](https://opensource.org/license/gpl-3.0) expressly address combining the two, while retaining each part's applicable terms. This permission is not proof that every other dependency or distribution condition has been satisfied.

For each actual binary release, the project must identify its source revision and exact component set, preserve license/attribution and modification notices, and make the corresponding source available through an applicable license-compliant method. Where a binary is offered for download under section 6(d), provide equivalent source access and clear directions alongside it. Native corresponding source may require matching patches and build/install scripts; a wheel URL, generic upstream homepage or different source revision is not automatically sufficient. Preserve applicable output/producer notices as well.

The assembled companion Crosscript-third-party-0.1.0-5-materials-v4.tar.gz contains exact source archives, notices and component indexes with explicit per-component status. Availability in this local candidate is not a completed public distribution. The remaining materials and uncertain facts are tracked in [the AGPL execution checklist](docs/distribution-materials.md). There is no general requirement to hire a lawyer or buy an additional license when the existing licenses are followed; a specific unresolved permission or compatibility issue may still require clarification, a compatible replacement or an effective additional grant. No external authorization or completed all-stack compliance review is claimed here.

## Release packaging tools (not bundled in the App)

The headless DMG layout builder uses the unmodified Python sources of [ds_store 1.3.3](https://github.com/dmgbuild/ds_store) and [mac_alias 2.2.3](https://github.com/dmgbuild/mac_alias), both distributed under the MIT license. These tools generate `.DS_Store` and a volume-relative background alias during packaging; their modules are not copied into the App or the installation image, and users do not need to install them. Original full copyright/license notices are retained in [ds_store-LICENSE.txt](scripts/release/vendor/ds_store-LICENSE.txt) and [mac_alias-LICENSE.txt](scripts/release/vendor/mac_alias-LICENSE.txt). Exact source/distribution hashes, pinned versions and upstream provenance are recorded in [provenance.json](scripts/release/vendor/provenance.json). The complete source materials retain these build tools and their licenses.
